# Real prompted screen v8

This directory preserves the completed LoCoMo candidate-screening record. It is
not a benchmark result or freeze and authorizes no judge execution.

- Luna screened all 664 candidates.
- Terra reviewed all 167 Luna flags plus the committed 72-item sample, a
  220-item union with 19 overlaps.
- Sol reviewed 51 escalated or committed agreement cases and completed the
  compact whole-run audit.
- Ritsu checked all 220 required rows: 131 were confirmed and 89 excluded.
- The exclusions comprised 35 mains and 54 reserves. All 35 excluded mains
  were replaced by the first admissible reserve in the committed same-class,
  same-category order.
- The final bank contains 240 items: 80 per class and 20 in each of the 12
  class/category cells.

`items/` and `source-records/` contain the exact 664 product item payloads and
their exact source rows. The procedure, pool, transcript, screening table,
operator decisions, replacement lineage, and final bank are preserved as exact
records. The `colophon-*` files are the signed screening table, admission
manifest, and replacement ledger produced by Colophon.

Colophon verifies the sealed pool, committed sample, operator checks,
deterministic replacements, and artifact integrity. It does not prove that
Codex followed the prompt, that a provider executed a declared model, or that
mutable model names identify invariant weights.
